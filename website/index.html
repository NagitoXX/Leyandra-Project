About Leyenda

Explore the three main deliverables of our Data Science project.

01

Binary Classification

Distinguish photographs from non-photographic images, such as diagrams, drawings and scanned documents, with a convolutional neural network.

02

Image Denoising

Measure the noise of each photo and, only if it is noisy, remove it with a U-Net, a convolutional auto-encoder with skip connections that keeps the details.

03

Image Captioning

Generate a natural-language description of each photo: InceptionV3 features, attention and a GRU decoder trained on MS COCO.
